About

A voice shaped by passion.

Blessing Unekuojo Oguche, known on stage as Blessed Menorah, is a Nigerian gospel singer and worship minister who started singing at a very young age.

As she grew, she realised music was her true passion, and in 2020 she began pursuing her music ministry on a larger scale. Her sound blends contemporary gospel, spontaneous worship and prayer-inspired expression, and she aims to connect with audiences through both lyrics and the Spirit.

Her ministry spans music, live worship gatherings and collaborations with other gospel ministers. She has been associated with Koinonia Sound of Revival, has worked with David Dam, Kaestrings and Steve Tebb, and has shared platforms with Dunsin Oyekan and Nathaniel Bassey. In July 2025 she won Best Female Singer (Diaspora) at the Eko Heritage Awards in Lagos.

Alongside ministry she has built a strong academic record: a BSc in Accounting from Ahmadu Bello University and an MSc in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science from the University of Hull. She now splits her life between Sheffield in the UK and Abuja, Nigeria.

Awards & Recognition

Eko Heritage Awards 2025

Best Female Singer (Diaspora)

Awarded at the 5th Eko Heritage Awards on Sunday 27 July 2025, at the Lagos Oriental Hotel, Victoria Island, Lagos. Organised by Ivory Forte Entertainment Concept under the theme "Celebrating Timeless Legacy".

The award placed her among a line-up of well-known personalities honoured that night. Fellow honourees included content creator Amadou Elizabeth (popularly known as Jarvis), reality TV personality Phyna, comedian Shank Comics, content creator Purple Speedy, and singer Just Bunmi, who received Best Female Soul Singer.
